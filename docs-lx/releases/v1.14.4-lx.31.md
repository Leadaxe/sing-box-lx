## 🐛 Fixed

### 🔀 Tunnel with `detour` on a selector stayed on the old node after switching

- Before: `masque`, `trusttunnel`, `hysteria2` and `tuic` brought their session up inside the first user connection that needed it; the router marked that connection external, so a selector without `interrupt_exist_connections` never closed the underlay — the tunnel kept running through the previous node until its session died, and a restart was the only cure.
- Now: the session underlay is dialled as an internal connection; a selector switch closes it and the next dial goes through the new node. WireGuard/AWG and Tailscale are not affected. Unit-tested; no live run yet. (SPEC 152)

- OpenVPN / OpenConnect: UDP sockets are closed and reopened when the tunnel addresses change; our 15 s connect timeout in the netstack is kept.
- OpenConnect: GPST re-authenticates on HTTP 200, AnyConnect CSD completion, F5 VPN options parsing.
- TUN (sing-tun fork rebased on the new pin): strict route without kernel IPv6 support; default route with RAS connections on Windows.
- naiveproxy v154.0.8037.49-2. (SPEC 154)
