VLESS nodes that use both `flow: xtls-rprx-vision` and VLESS Encryption (`mlkem768x25519plus…`) now connect, over any transport, XHTTP included. Before, every such node failed at once with `vision: not a valid supported TLS connection`, although Vision alone and the encryption alone both worked.

VLESS-узлы, у которых одновременно `flow: xtls-rprx-vision` и VLESS-шифрование (`mlkem768x25519plus…`), теперь подключаются на любом транспорте, включая XHTTP. Раньше каждый такой узел сразу падал с `vision: not a valid supported TLS connection`, хотя Vision отдельно и шифрование отдельно работали.

## 🐛 Fixed

### 🔐 VLESS: Vision on top of VLESS Encryption

- Was: a node with both `flow: xtls-rprx-vision` and `encryption` failed on every connection with `initialize vision: vision: not a valid supported TLS connection: *encryption.CommonConn`; URL tests showed it as unreachable. Seen on `tcp`+REALITY and `xhttp`+REALITY.
- Now: Vision runs on the encryption layer the way Xray does it. It reads the layer's buffered data, and after the inner TLS 1.3 handshake it copies directly to the conn beneath the layer. Any transport works; checked against live nodes on `tcp` and `xhttp` with REALITY, including a 20 MB HTTPS download. Nodes without Vision or without encryption behave as before. No new keys, wire unchanged. ([#29](https://github.com/Leadaxe/sing-box-lx/issues/29), [SPEC 105](https://github.com/Leadaxe/sing-box-lx/blob/lx/SPECS/TASKS/105-VISION_OVER_VLESS_ENCRYPTION/SPEC.md))
- Apps that drop `flow` from XHTTP nodes should keep it when the node sets `encryption`: Vision over XHTTP works only in that combination.
