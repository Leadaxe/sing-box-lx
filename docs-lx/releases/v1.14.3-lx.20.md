## 🆕 What's new

### 📥 `lxd update` prints download progress

- Before: after `update: downloading <asset>` nothing was written until the archive had been received and verified — on a router tens of seconds with a line that does not move.
- Now: while the archive streams in, `update: downloading <asset>: 3.2 / 14.1 MiB (23%)` is printed at most once per second; the total comes from the server's `Content-Length` (GitHub release assets have one), without it only the received bytes are shown. A download that finishes within one second prints no progress line; `sha256 match` follows as before.
- Once per second, not per percent: the output is a file the daemon serves by its tail and the launcher polls once a second, so a 15 MiB archive leaves tens of lines, not thousands.
- Not affected: the download itself, the checksum, the swap, the restart and the health steps; the `installed …, available …` line the launcher parses. (SPEC 129)

## 🧰 Under the hood

- Base: sing-box `v1.14.3`, zero drift from `upstream/stable`; submodules and dependencies unchanged since lx.19. Go 1.26.8.
- Verified by `TestDownloadProgress` (injected clock: silent within a tick, one line per tick, bytes-only format without a total).
